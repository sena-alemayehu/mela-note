# Mela Note — User Guide

Welcome to **Mela Note**, your fast, beautiful, and secure workspace for personal thoughts, checklists, and attachments.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### 1.1 Creating an Account
1. Open Mela Note and navigate to **Sign Up**.
2. Enter your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and choose a secure **Password** (minimum 8 characters).
3. Click **Create account**.
4. Your account is secured with Argon2id cryptographic password hashing and enterprise-grade session protection.

### 1.2 Signing In
1. Enter your email and password.
2. Click **Sign in**.
3. If you type the wrong password 5 times in a row, your account will be temporarily locked for 15 minutes to protect against unauthorized access attempts.

### 1.3 Language Selection (English / አማርኛ)
- You can switch between **English** and **Amharic (አማርኛ)** anytime using the language selector located in the top bar. All interface buttons, labels, and messages will immediately update.

---

## 2. Managing Your Notes

### 2.1 Creating a Note
1. Click the **+ Create note** button from the dashboard header or notes section.
2. Choose between:
   - **📄 Text note**: For free-form thoughts, memos, articles, or meeting notes.
   - **☑️ Checklist**: For to-do lists, shopping lists, or project tasks.
3. Enter a title for your note.

### 2.2 Working with Checklists
1. When creating or editing a checklist note, type your task in the input box and press **Enter** (or click **+ Add**).
2. Click the checkbox next to any item to tick or untick it. Ticked items display a strike-through and update the progress indicator (e.g. `2 / 5 completed`).
3. Click the **✕** button on any item to delete it.

### 2.3 Note Colors & Pinning
- **Color Palette**: Choose from Google Keep-style soft pastel tones (Default White, Warm Yellow, Soft Green, Sky Blue, Lavender, Blush Pink, Peach Orange) to visually organize your ideas.
- **Pinning (📌)**: Click the pin icon in the editor or on the note card. Pinned notes stay fixed at the top of your workspace.
- **Favorites (☆)**: Click the favorite button on any note card to add it to your Favorites collection for quick access.

### 2.4 Tags and Labels
1. In the note editor, under the **Labels** section, type a tag name (e.g., `work`, `ideas`, `urgent`) and press **Enter** or click **+ Add tag**.
2. Tags appear as chips on your note card.
3. In the dashboard, click any tag filter chip at the top to display only notes with that tag.

### 2.5 Autosave
- While you write, Mela Note automatically saves your draft 1.5 seconds after you pause typing.
- A status badge in the editor header displays **● Saving changes...** followed by **✓ All changes saved**, so you never lose your progress.

---

## 3. Attachments & Media

### 3.1 Adding Files
- In the note editor, you can drag and drop files directly onto the attachment zone, or click **Browse** to select files from your device.
- Supports **Images** (JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, SVG), **Documents** (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Text, CSV), and **Audio**.
- Each file can be up to **25 MB**.

### 3.2 Viewing & Downloading
- Click the attachment indicator on any note card to preview the attached files in the built-in lightbox viewer.
- Click **Download** to safely download any attachment to your device.

---

## 4. Organizing & Recovery

### 4.1 Archiving Notes
- Click **Archive** on any active note to store it out of your everyday list while keeping it safe and searchable.

### 4.2 Trash & Restoration
- When you delete a note, it is moved to the **Trash**.
- Open the **Trash** tab from the quick access section to review deleted notes.
- Click **Restore** to return any note to your active notes list.
- Click **Delete permanently** if you want to permanently erase the note and its stored attachment files from the server.
